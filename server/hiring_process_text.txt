Section 1: Introduction
Why Do We Hire International Labour?
●​ The Problem
Australia is one of the world’s largest mining and oil hubs, and falls short of local skilled
workers, in the mining, oil, and construction industries for the following but not limited to this
reason :
●​ Majority of the companies in the aforementioned industries carry out operations
in remote, regional areas of Australia with little to no human inhabitants and will
require flying in and flying out (FIFO) of workers from operational areas, and as
such majority of employable native Australians do not find the work conditions in
the areas of operation attractive, hence the resolution by these companies to seek
for labour overseas.
●​ Our Solution
Blue Collar Recruitment Pty Ltd aims to solve this shortage by acquiring, vetting, and training of
foreign talent not resident in Australia, for their Australian client companies.
To ensure that the applicants meet the rigorous Australian standards for mining, construction and
oil blue collar jobs, Blue Collar Recruitment Pty Ltd obtained a partnership with Cert Hero
Registered Training Organisation to provide training to deserving applicants.
What is in it for you?
The benefits for a potential employee are, but not limited to zero cost migration to Australia (Tickets,
Visa, Flights, all sponsored directly or indirectly by the employer), and great job benefits offered in the
mining, construction, and oil industries. The benefits are highlighted below:
1.​ A stable mining, oil or construction industry job in Australia.
2.​ Employer sponsored Positive Skills Assessment with the Trade Recognition Australia, needed
for 482 visa subclass sponsorships and application.
3.​ Employer sponsored medical fitness test for visa sponsorship and application.
4.​ Employer Visa Sponsorship (482 Visa subclass; skills in demand visa).
5.​ Employer Sponsored Flight Tickets to Australia.
6.​ Free tickets (certification) training, with refund if expenditure was incurred by applicants
previously.
7.​ Robust Compensation Structures and Remuneration Advantages.
