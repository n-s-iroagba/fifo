### **A.4 Visa Sponsorship**

The employer acts as the primary visa sponsor for the Subclass 482 (Skills in Demand) visa. In practice this means the employer:

* Lodges the nomination for your specific role and position through its ImmiAccount.  
* Pays for your skills assessment.  
* Pays the visa processing fees.  
* Generates and gives you the Transaction Reference Number (TRN) that links your visa application to the nomination.  
* Arranges your flight tickets and your reception in Australia.

No external migration agents are used. The client company's Human Resource (HR) onboarding team, which doubles as the overseas employee team, guides you through the visa application, delivers your flight tickets and receives you on arrival.

**What this means for you:** you do not pay the nomination, skills assessment or visa processing fees, but you remain responsible for gathering your documents, completing your own application and meeting each eligibility requirement below.

### **A.4.1 The Visa Class**

**Subclass 482 – Skills in Demand Visa**, available under two streams:

* **Specialist Skills Stream:** minimum salary of **\$146,576 AUD** (effective 1 July 2026). Can be processed in as little as **7 working days**.  
* **Core Skills Stream:** minimum salary of **\$79,423 AUD** (effective 1 July 2026).

Key points:

* **Pathway to permanent residency:** after 2 years on the 482 visa you become eligible for the Subclass 186 (Employer Nomination Scheme) permanent residency visa.  
* **Lodgement window:** you must lodge your visa application within **12 months** of the nomination being approved, or the nomination expires.  
* **Early lodgement:** you may lodge as soon as your employer gives you the TRN, even before the nomination is approved.  
* **Policy changes:** thresholds, fees and test requirements can change. Always confirm with the Department of Home Affairs (immi.homeaffairs.gov.au).

### **A.4.2 The Visa Sponsor**

**Maca Ltd Pty Ltd** (or the client company, through the Blue Collar Recruitment Pty Ltd HR/Onboarding team) is the sponsor.

**The sponsor is responsible for:**

* Lodging the employer nomination application via ImmiAccount.  
* Providing you with the TRN (the Department of Home Affairs generates it once the nomination is lodged).

**The sponsor is not responsible for:** completing your documents, taking your tests or obtaining your police certificates. Those are your actions, set out in A.4.3.

### **A.4.3 General Sponsorship Eligibility Requirements for the Employee**

You must satisfy all four requirements before your visa application is lodged (the English test must be no more than 3 years old at lodgement).

**1\. Positive Skills Assessment (TRA)**

* A formal validation of your qualifications and work experience for your nominated occupation.  
* Trades are assessed through Trades Recognition Australia (TRA).  
* Upload all tickets (trade certificates) acquired from Cert Hero, plus identity documents, certified qualifications and transcripts, signed employer reference letters (job title, duties, exact dates, weekly hours) with payslips, tax records or bank statements, and an up-to-date CV.  
* Processing takes about 4 weeks. The employer pays the fee.

**2\. English Language Proficiency (IELTS 5.0+ or equivalent, or an exemption)**

* Minimum scores: IELTS 5.0 in each band; PTE Academic 33/36/29/24 (Listening/Reading/Writing/Speaking); other approved tests include TOEFL iBT, OET, CELPIP General, LANGUAGECERT Academic and Michigan English Test.  
* No remote-proctored or at-home tests are accepted. The test must be taken within 3 years of lodgement.  
* Exemptions: 5 years of full-time secondary or higher study taught in English; a valid passport from the UK, USA, Canada, New Zealand or Ireland; guaranteed annual earnings of at least \$96,400 AUD; or an occupation licence that required equal or higher English.

**3\. Health Examination (HAP ID)**

* Obtain a HAP ID through the 'My Health Declarations' system, then book with an approved panel physician (not a local GP).  
* Bring your passport and medical history. Expect a physical exam, chest X-ray, blood tests and any extra tests for your age, nationality or occupation.  
* Results are valid for 12 months.

**4\. Character Checks (Police Certificates and Form 80\)**

* Police certificates from every country where you lived 12 months or more in the past 10 years since turning 16, with certified translations where not in English.  
* Declare all criminal conduct, including pending charges.  
* Complete Form 80, and Form 1563 if requested. Include a military certificate (Form 1399\) if applicable.  
* Police certificates are generally valid for 12 months from issue.

**Document rules for everything above:** certify all copies, translate all non-English documents through an accredited translator, and make sure your name is identical on every document.

### **4\. VISA APPLICATION (Step 4\)**

The work below follows the General Process Overview. Several actions can run in parallel once the nomination is lodged.

**Date:**

**Applicant's responsibilities:**

**Applicant's requirements:**

**The company's responsibilities:**

* Lodge the employer nomination application via ImmiAccount for your specific role and position.  
* Provide you with the TRN once the Department of Home Affairs generates it.  
* Keep track of the 12-month nomination window.

#### **4.2 Skills Assessment**

**Date:**

**Applicant's responsibilities:**

* Register with Trades Recognition Australia (https\://www\.tradesrecognitionaustralia.com) or the relevant assessing authority.  
* Upload all tickets (trade certificates) acquired from Cert Hero.  
* Submit the application through the authority's online portal.  
* Wait for the outcome (about 4 weeks) and share the result with the employer.

**Applicant's requirements:**

* Passport bio-data page (clear colour copy) and passport-sized photograph.  
* Certified copies of degree, diploma and trade certificates, and official academic transcripts.  
* Signed reference letters on company letterhead stating job title, detailed duties, exact start and end dates, and weekly hours.  
* Supporting evidence: payslips, tax records, bank statements showing salary deposits.  
* Up-to-date CV, and change-of-name documents if applicable.  
* Certified translations for non-English documents.

**The company's responsibilities:**

* Pay the assessment fee (typically \$795 – \$1,500 AUD).  
* Guide you on which authority and occupation apply.

#### **4.3 Visa Processing Fees**

**Applicant's requirements:**
